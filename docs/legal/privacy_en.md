# BJJ Bul – Privacy Policy

Version 1.2 · Effective 11 October 2026

## 1. Who we are
BJJ Bul is responsible for the personal data processed through the app. Contact: admin@bjjbul.com

## 2. What we collect
- **Account:** email address, or your Apple ID identifier (with Apple, your email can be a private relay address). Your name, if you choose to share it.
- **Profile:** display name, avatar, belt, stripes, home gym, and when you accepted these terms.
- **Content:** posts, photos, comments, likes, follows, gym edit suggestions, reports and blocks.
- **Gym reviews:** your star rating and review text for a gym.
- **Open mats:** open mat sessions you post (gym, time, style, fee, notes).
- **Drop-in requests:** the gym, preferred date or class and your message.
- **Gym submissions:** new gyms and location corrections you submit (name, contact details you enter, address and map pin).
- **Training log:** your training sessions (date, duration, type, gym, techniques, sparring rounds, notes, rating).
- **Followed gyms and notifications:** gyms you follow, your notification preferences, in-app notifications, and your device's push token (an identifier Apple issues for sending notifications to your device).
- **Ask BJJ (AI chat):** your questions, the AI's answers, and a daily message counter.
- **Location:** only on your device, to show nearby gyms. We don't store your location. A pin you place when submitting a gym is stored as part of the submission.
- **Technical:** basic logs needed to run the service securely.

## 3. Why we use it (legal basis)
- To provide the app and your account, including the training log, reviews, open mats, drop-in requests, notifications and Ask BJJ (performance of a contract).
- For safety and moderation: reviewing reports, reviews, open mats and gym submissions, and acting on abuse (legitimate interest; legal obligations).
- To keep the gym directory accurate (legitimate interest).
- To send push notifications, only if you allow them on your device. You can turn them off in iOS Settings or in the app's notification settings.

We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

## 4. Who can see it
- **Public to other users:** your posts, comments, display name, belt and home gym; your gym reviews (with your display name and rating); and open mats you post. Users you block can't see your posts, comments or reviews.
- **Shared with the gym:** a drop-in request is visible to you and to the owner of that gym's page in the app (along with your display name). It's also visible to our administrators.
- **Private to you:** your training log, followed gyms, notification preferences, notifications, push tokens and Ask BJJ conversations. Only you can see them in the app. Our administrators can access the database only for operation, security and support.
- **Moderators only:** reports and gym submissions.
- **Shop (marketplace):** when you send an order request, the items, your name, phone, delivery details (Econt/Speedy office or address) and notes are shared with that partner store only, which acts as an independent controller for fulfilling the order. Partners and our administrators see the order; other users don't. When you tap "Buy on the shop's website", we log the click (product, time, a random reference code and your account if signed in) to count referrals; the shop's own privacy policy applies on its site. Orders are kept for accounting (up to 5 years, as required by law); you can ask us to delete the rest.

## 5. Ask BJJ (AI chat)
- **What's sent:** when you send a message, we send it to our AI provider together with up to 12 earlier messages from the same conversation, instructions for the assistant, and relevant public app data (for example gyms, competitions and open mats that match your question). We don't send your email, your name or your account identifier.
- **Provider:** the AI provider is xAI or OpenAI, acting as our processor. It processes the data only to generate the answer, under its API terms, which don't allow it to use API data to train its models.
- **Transfers outside the EU:** the provider may process data outside the EU/EEA (for example in the USA). Such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards under the GDPR: the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
- **History:** your conversations are stored in our database (ai_conversations and ai_messages) so the assistant can follow the conversation. You can delete your conversations in Ask BJJ (Delete history). They are also deleted when you delete your account.
- **Usage counter:** to keep the service fair and affordable, we count the messages you send each day (limit: 30 per day). The counter keeps only the date and the number.
- **Don't share sensitive data:** please don't share health information (such as injuries or medical conditions) or other sensitive personal data in the chat. Ask BJJ doesn't give medical advice.
- **Accuracy:** AI answers can be inaccurate; check important information.

## 6. Processors
- **Supabase:** our backend (database, authentication, file storage and server functions).
- **Apple:** Sign in with Apple and push notifications (Apple Push Notification service).
- **Maps:** map data comes from Apple Maps and OpenStreetMap.
- **AI:** Ask BJJ answers are generated by xAI or OpenAI (see section 5).
- **Instagram:** if a gym page shows an embedded Instagram post, it is loaded from Instagram (Meta), which may process your device's technical data under its own policy.

## 7. Retention
We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account (Profile → Delete account), we delete your profile, posts, photos, comments, likes, follows, blocks, suggestions, reviews, drop-in requests, training log, followed gyms, notification preferences, notifications, push tokens, Ask BJJ conversations and usage counters.
- **Kept without your name:** open mats you posted and gym submissions you made are kept without being linked to you, because they're part of the shared calendar and directory.
- **Reports:** we may keep reports about abusive content for as long as needed for safety or legal reasons.
- **Push tokens:** tokens that Apple reports as no longer valid are deleted automatically.

## 8. Your rights (GDPR)
You have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Email admin@bjjbul.com to use them. You can also complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (www.cpdp.bg).

## 9. Children
The app isn't intended for children under 16 without consent from a parent or guardian.

## 10. Changes
We'll tell you in the app about significant changes to this policy.
